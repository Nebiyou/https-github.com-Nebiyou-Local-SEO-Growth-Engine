# Grade 5 Math Standardized Testing — All 50 States + DC (2025–26 School Year)

Research reference supporting `SYLLABUS.md`. Verified against state Department of
Education sources, July 2026.

## The big picture

- **~45 of 51 jurisdictions** test grade 5 math content that maps directly to the
  **Common Core State Standards (CCSS)** — either as-is, or as a state-branded
  rebrand/revision (e.g., "Tennessee Academic Standards," "PA Core," "Iowa Core").
- **Six jurisdictions use genuinely divergent standards** with grade-5 content beyond
  CCSS: **Texas (TEKS), Virginia (2023 SOL), Florida (B.E.S.T.), Minnesota (2007
  standards), Oklahoma (OAS 2022), South Carolina (2025 SCCCR)** — plus structural
  divergence in **Nebraska (2022 CCR)** and **North Dakota (2023 standards)**.
- Extra topics those states test at grade 5 (all absent from CCSS grade 5):
  - **Mean / median / mode / range** — FL, MN (no mode), OK, VA
  - **Probability** (likelihood 0–1, sample spaces, tree diagrams, counting principle) — VA, SC, ND
  - **Personal financial literacy** (taxes, gross/net income, payment methods, budgets) — TX; explicit money problems — FL
  - **Extra data displays** (stem-and-leaf, scatterplots, frequency tables, double-bar & line graphs) — TX, OK, MN, FL, OR
  - **Variables, simple equations & inequalities** — TX, VA, OK, MN, NE
  - **Prime factorization** — VA
  - **3-D figure classification & nets** — FL, OK
  - **Triangle classification by sides/angles** — TX, VA, FL

## Test families (identical content structure within family)

- **Smarter Balanced (SBAC)**: CA, CT, DE, HI, ID, NV, NH*, OR*, SD, WA (+ MI claims model)
- **PARCC heritage** (Major Content / Reasoning / Modeling subclaims): CO CMAS, DC CAPE, IL IAR, LA LEAP, MD MCAP
- **Cambium/Cognia/Pearson/DRC state-branded CCSS tests**: most remaining states
- **Fully independent**: TX STAAR, VA SOL, FL FAST, MN MCA, OK OSTP, SC READY

## Question formats used across state tests (grade 5 math)

Multiple choice (4 options) · multi-select ("choose all that apply") · numeric/equation
entry (incl. fractions) · drag-and-drop classification & ordering · inline drop-down
choice · number-line and coordinate-plane plotting · hot spot · graph construction.
STAAR caps multiple choice at ~75% of items; Smarter Balanced adds performance tasks.

## Timing caveats

- Minnesota tests its 2007 standards through 2026–27; MCA-IV (2022 standards) starts 2027–28.
- South Carolina's 2025 standards are first fully tested in 2025–26.
- Texas HB 8 replaces STAAR with a three-window system starting 2027–28.
- Virginia's 2023 SOLs have been live since spring 2025.
